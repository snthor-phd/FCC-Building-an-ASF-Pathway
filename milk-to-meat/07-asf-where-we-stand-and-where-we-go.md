# **Faircreek ASF — Where We Stand and Where We Go**

Nov 14, 2026 · Steven Thorsen · working document for the Nov 16 meeting

## **BLUF**

Faircreek's adult formation now runs on one frame: milk to meat, from Hebrews 5:11–6:2. Everyone starts with the gospel at Starting Point, learns the milk (the six foundations of Hebrews 6:1–2 and the four devotions of Acts 2:42), and grows by practice into meat, the same truths trained into judgment, until they can pass them on (2 Tim 2:2). Two pathways run on that one map: new believers start at the foundation, and long-time members start with a conversation. Foundations carries the milk for newcomers, and LDP carries leaders, with meat modules braided in.

The structure's in place. What's missing is content and a few calls only Matt and the elders can make. Two milk modules and all nine meat modules are still unwritten, and the biggest gate is how Faircreek teaches baptism and the Lord's Supper. Nov 16's job is to get those calls (decisions 1–7 below), name the first course and date, and agree to a Lent 2027 pilot that ends in Easter baptisms.

## **Key terms**

* **Milk:** the first truths of the faith, "the first principles of the oracles of God" (Heb 5:12). Everyone starts here, and no one outgrows it.  
* **Meat (solid food):** the same truths, trained in by practice until they shape judgment and can be taught (Heb 5:14). It's depth, not a harder menu.  
* **Topics \#0–\#10:** the gospel (\#0), the six foundations of Hebrews 6:1–2 (\#1–\#6: repentance, faith, baptisms, laying on of hands, resurrection, judgment) and the four devotions of Acts 2:42 (\#7–\#10: the apostles' teaching, fellowship, breaking of bread, prayers). Generosity (\#11) and witness and service (\#12) are proposed.  
* **Marks:** where a person sits on each topic: Milk (can say it), Growing (practices it), Meat (discerns with it), Multiplying (passes it on). The briefing calls the same four steps Milk, Practice, Meat and Sent.  
* **Pathway A:** the path for new believers, foundation first.  
* **Pathway B:** the path for long-time members, starting with an entry conversation.  
* **Entry conversation:** a sponsor-led talk, plus a few weeks of observed practice, that places a person on each topic. It's a conversation, not a test.  
* **Sponsor:** the mature believer who walks with someone through the path, as in the early church's catechumenate.  
* **Foundations:** Faircreek's six three-week courses (54 sessions) from The Irresistible Church Network; the milk spine for Pathway A.  
* **LDP:** Faircreek's Leadership Development Program, three phases of three modules over about nine months. The Discipleship Flywheel calls it Leadership Development Groups (LDGs).  
* **Meat modules (M1–M9):** the nine solid-food modules on the map. Titles and core texts exist; none are written.  
* **Align:** Faircreek's twice-yearly leaders' gathering, where LDP graduates are commissioned.  
* **The house:** Faircreek's Foyer, Living Room, Kitchen and Neighborhood graphic ("For the One in 2026"); the Discipleship Flywheel draws it as a cycle.

## **Where we stand**

### **The frame**

Hebrews 5:12 is the burden that started this: people who "by this time ought to be teachers" still need milk. Years in church don't make anyone mature; practice does, "by reason of use" (5:14). So the map treats milk and meat as the same truths at different depths, and the practices are how milk becomes meat. The gospel comes before any milk (1 Cor 15:3–4), and that's Starting Point's job. Two guardrails hold it together: sanctification is God's work, so practices answer grace and never earn it; and the map describes growth without ranking people.

### **What's in place**

* **Starting Point:** the gospel first step, in the Foyer.  
* **Foundations:** six courses in this order: Follow Jesus, Story of the Bible, Who God Is, New Identity, Spiritual Habits, Read the Bible Well. Two teaching teams are serving (the Mallares and the Thorsens), and four couples are invited.  
* **Seven practices over two years:** Year One, Rooted (Apprenticeship, Scripture, Prayer, Community); Year Two, Sent (Serving, Witness, Generosity). Sabbath, solitude and fasting are under review.  
* **LDP:** groups of four with a facilitator and a mentor, a month serving in a ministry each phase, and commencement at Align.  
* **The house:** the note on our copy marks "develop depth" as the part to build, and that's where the meat modules go.  
* **Settled guardrails:** Faircreek's Statement of Faith governs, with the Nicene Creed and the Westminster Confession as reference. The lead pastor is male, and elders serve under a complementarian philosophy.  
* **Roles:** Matt owns the purpose, the theological boundaries and the invitation. Steve coordinates discovery, curriculum alignment and documentation. Teaching teams teach, model the practices and hand back notes.

### **What the map shows**

* **Milk is mostly covered** for the gospel, faith, Scripture, fellowship and prayer, through Starting Point and Foundations.  
* **The ordinances are practiced but not taught.** Faircreek baptizes and takes communion regularly, but no session teaches what either means, and there's no ordinance of laying on of hands. Hebrews 6:2 calls these a teaching, not just acts.  
* **Last things are thin.** Judgment barely appears, and the resurrection of the body gets less than "heaven as home."  
* **The meat column is nearly empty.** The nine modules are titles and texts only, so Pathway B has nothing to run yet.  
* **LDP covers practice, not doctrine.** It's strong on Bible method (\#7), fellowship (\#8) and gifts, but M2 through M6, the doctrinal core of the meat, aren't in it.  
* **No measure of formation.** Nothing yet tells growth apart from attendance.

## **Where we're going**

### **One map, two pathways**

Both pathways run on the same eleven topics and end at the same place: commissioned to multiply.

**Pathway A: new believer, foundation first**

1. Welcome and Starting Point, with a sponsor from day one.  
2. Foundations Course 1, How to Follow Jesus (repentance and faith).  
3. New milk module, "Baptism and the Table": what baptism and the Lord's Supper mean, and belonging and blessing. It closes with baptism, so baptism moves to the front.  
4. Foundations Courses 2–4: Story of the Bible, Who God Is, New Identity.  
5. New milk module, "Hope and Account": the raised body, judgment and assurance.  
6. Foundations Courses 5–6: Spiritual Habits, Read the Bible Well.  
7. Year Two, Sent, with the sponsor, then an entry conversation to place them for meat.

**Pathway B: long-time member, assessed first**

1. Entry conversation with a sponsor or elder couple.  
2. Milk only where it's needed: the single Foundations week or milk module that covers a weak topic.  
3. Meat modules chosen from the placement.  
4. A practice paired with each module, braided into LDP.  
5. Commissioning with laying on of hands at Align.

### **Where formation happens**

The map's too long as one list, so we'll sort each piece into one of three settings. These are first guesses to react to.

* **Church groups:** Starting Point, Foundations and the two milk modules; the Lord's Supper, corporate prayer and fellowship.  
* **Mentors:** the sponsor and entry conversation; LDP; the meat modules' paired practice; commissioning at Align.  
* **On your own:** daily Scripture and prayer (God Show Me, God Teach Me, God Change Me), reading plans and rest.

### **LDP on the path**

We keep every LDP module and make three changes:

* **A milk check before Phase One.** Read the "From Milk to Meat" chapter, fill in its self-check, and hold an entry conversation. Any gap gets the Foundations week or milk module that covers it. Baptism happens here, since the Leadership Agreement already requires it.  
* **A meat module braided into each module.** Phase One takes M7 (entry), the laying-on-of-hands foundation and M1, with M8 begun. Phase Two takes M3, M2 and M4. Phase Three takes M5, M9 (begun) and M6.  
* **Commencement becomes commissioning** at Align, with laying on of hands, and only after the evidence is seen (1 Tim 5:22).

The cost is time: groups either meet twice a month, or LDP stretches from about nine months to about twelve.

### **Leadership**

Leaders come from the meat end of the map: not a novice, able to teach, tested first, and not commissioned in a hurry (1 Tim 3:2, 6, 10; 5:22). The map gives selection a record of watched growth instead of years served. It's evidence, not a scorecard, and the Leadership Agreement already sets the character standards. The path is the same for women and men (Titus 2:3–5; Acts 18:26). Elders and pastors are drawn from people the church has watched move from milk to meat and into multiplying.

### **Roadmap**

1. **Nov 16:** get decisions 1–7, name the first course and date, and agree to the Lent pilot.  
2. **Late Nov to Dec 2026:** write "Baptism and the Table" once the practice is set. Turn the three-way sort into a one-page map. Name sponsors and a lead and backup teaching team.  
3. **Jan to early Feb 2027:** hold entry conversations with the teaching teams (Pathway B's first test). Brief 4–6 sponsors. Draft the first meat modules LDP Phase One needs (M7 and M1).  
4. **Lent pilot, Feb 10 to Mar 28, 2027:** Starting Point, then How to Follow Jesus (three weeks) and "Baptism and the Table" (three weeks), ending in baptisms on Easter Sunday, Mar 28\.  
5. **Apr to Aug 2027:** debrief within two weeks, check the marks before and after, write "Hope and Account" and the next meat modules, braid them into the next LDP cohort, and plan a fall 2027 rollout, perhaps alongside a sermon series on Hebrews 5 and 6\.

## **Glossary**

* **Align:** Faircreek's twice-yearly leaders' gathering.  
* **Catechumenate:** the early church's path from inquiry through instruction and baptism to teaching after baptism, with a sponsor at each step.  
* **Complementarian:** the view that the elder office is reserved to qualified men, while women and men share the work of ministry.  
* **Discipleship Flywheel:** Faircreek's house graphic drawn as a cycle.  
* **Entry conversation, Foundations, LDP, Marks, Meat, Milk, Pathway A, Pathway B, Sponsor, Topics:** see Key terms.  
* **Ex officio seat:** a board seat that comes with a position; under Ohio law it has no vote unless the regulations say otherwise.  
* **Learning Lab:** LDP's month of hands-on service in a Faircreek ministry.  
* **Leadership Agreement:** the covenant Faircreek leaders sign.  
* **Starting Point:** Faircreek's first step, where people hear the gospel.  
* **Statement of Faith:** Faircreek's doctrinal statement, which every leader signs.
