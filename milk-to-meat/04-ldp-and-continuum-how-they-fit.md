# LDP and our milk-to-meat continuum: how they fit

Nov 14, 2026 · Steven Thorsen

**BLUF:** The Leadership Development Program (LDP) is Faircreek's Kitchen-room leadership track. It's strong on self-leadership, Bible-reading method, team culture and alignment with Faircreek. It's thin on doctrine: it teaches almost none of the Hebrews 6:1–2 foundations at milk or meat level. So it doesn't replace the meat modules. It sits beside them as the leadership and alignment layer. It also settles three things we've had open:

- **Where meat goes:** the Discipleship Flywheel in the manual is the house graphic as a cycle. "Develop Depth" is the arc from Kitchen to Neighborhood, so that's where meat goes.  
- **Doctrinal guardrails:** Faircreek's Statement of Faith governs, since every leader signs it.  
- **Character check:** the Leadership Agreement is the check the map itself can't make.

## Terms used here

- **LDP:** Faircreek's Leadership Development Program, the subject of the course manual.  
- **LDG:** Leadership Development Group. North Point's monthly leader group; the Faircreek flywheel lists "Leadership Development Groups" in the Kitchen. At Faircreek, that's LDP.  
- **Pathway B:** our path for long-time members: an entry conversation, milk only where it's needed, then meat modules M1–M9 and commissioning.  
- **Meat modules (M1–M9):** the nine solid-food modules in the continuum map.  
- **Continuum topics \#0–\#10:** the gospel, the six foundations of Hebrews 6:1–2, and the four devotions of Acts 2:42.  
- **OIA:** Observation, Interpretation, Application. It's Howard Hendricks's Bible-study method, which the manual teaches through North Point's *Methods* series.  
- **Core vs peripheral doctrine:** the manual's line between beliefs essential to Christianity (core) and beliefs where Christians differ (peripheral).

## What the LDP is

LDP runs 3 phases of 3 modules each, roughly nine months at one meeting a month. Each group has no more than four people, plus a facilitator and a mentor. Learning Labs place each person in a Faircreek ministry for a month. Graduates are commissioned at Align, the twice-yearly leaders' gathering.

| Phase | Module 1 | Module 2 | Module 3 |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 1\. Lead yourself well | Bible reading (God Show Me / Teach Me / Change Me; OIA); Stanley's Five Faith Catalysts | Spiritual gifts (Greg Trout) and temperament | Groeschel, *Dangerous Prayers* (Search me, Break me, Send me) |
| 2\. A leader others want to follow | Leadership Posture (Humble, Intentional, Teachable, FOR); Lencioni; Tony Morgan's theology of leadership | Working Genius; Maxwell | Anders, *30 Days to Understanding the Bible*; conflict (Matthew 18\) |
| 3\. Alignment with other leaders | Who the church is for; core vs peripheral doctrine; Apostles' Creed; Core Beliefs Assessment | DNA Alignment; *7 Practices*; mission, vision, Discipleship Flywheel | Leadership Agreement; Statement of Faith; personal leadership mission statement |

About a third of the reading is general leadership material (Lencioni, Maxwell, Working Genius). The rest is Bible method, prayer, gifts and Faircreek's own documents.

The proposed \#11 Generosity and \#12 Witness and service are already in LDP. The Leadership Agreement asks leaders to Give, Serve and Love (group), and Faircreek measures engagement as Give, Serve, Connect and Invite. That supports adding both topics to the continuum.

## What it settles or sharpens

1. **The flywheel confirms our house reading.** The Kitchen holds "Leadership Development Groups" and "SERVE 3 \- Leadership," and the arc out of it is "Develop Depth." That's the spot slide 6 already marks for the meat modules.  
2. **Doctrinal guardrails: Faircreek's Statement of Faith governs.** Leaders sign it, so the map's Westminster Confession of Faith (WCF) check works as a reference, not the standard. Where the two differ, we follow Faircreek. They differ on baptism (Faircreek: believers, by immersion; WCF allows infants and sprinkling) and church government (Faircreek: autonomous local church led by elders). The Statement also takes positions we should honor in module content: "salvation cannot be lost," and a future reign on earth.  
3. **Character check.** The map says it can't measure character. The Leadership Agreement already does: private disciplines, believers baptism, Give/Serve/Love, a relational covenant, and standards on substance use, sexuality and social media. Our leader-selection section can point to it instead of inventing one.  
4. **Core vs peripheral is a ready tool.** LDP's chart is a good test for how hard each module presses a point. Its rule of thumb: on core doctrine, state your position plainly; on peripheral matters, ask questions and hold opinions loosely.  
5. **A gap inside LDP.** It requires baptism of leaders but teaches it nowhere, and it never covers the Table, resurrection or judgment. Our gap list still stands, and the manual reinforces it.  
6. **Gifts, and a link to Acts 3\.** Trout describes gifts as "something God does through us" when the moment is beyond us, citing Acts 4:13, the fishermen who spoke with authority. That's the scene right after our new M9 anchor, Acts 3:1–10. M9 can borrow his framing.  
7. **Format match.** Groups of four, a facilitator, a mentor, monthly meetings and paired labs. That's the same shape as the LDG and our sponsor model, and it lives out "meat through practice" (Heb 5:14). LDP belongs in the "mentors" bucket of the three-way sort.

## Where LDP fits on the path

The adult-path doc makes LDP the stage 5 (Multiplying) core track. The manual supports that for Phases 2 and 3\. Phase 1 (Bible method, gifts, prayer) is really stage 3 to 4 content. LDP's entry bar is character and service ("integrity, work ethic, servant attitude"), with no doctrine check. Two ways to wire it:

- **Sequence:** Pathway B meat modules first (at least M2–M6), then LDP. Doctrine comes before the leadership role.  
- **Braid:** run LDP as-is and braid a meat module into each LDP module. Phase 1 takes M7 (entry), the laying-on-of-hands foundation and M1 (with M8 begun); Phase 2 takes M3, M2 and M4; Phase 3 takes M5, M9 (begun) and M6; commencement at Align becomes M9's commissioning, with M8 and deeper M7 in the optional half-days. The LDP Milk-to-Meat Transformation Map lays this out module by module.

Our pick is the braid. It puts doctrine where leaders are already meeting. The cost is time: each module gains a meat reading and a practice, so groups either meet twice a month or LDP stretches from about nine months to about twelve.

## Recommendations

1. Keep LDP as the Kitchen's leadership track. Don't treat it as meat on its own.  
2. Count LDP Phase 1 M1 and Phase 2 M3 as M7's entry layer. M7 then adds deeper exegesis, such as the Greek/Hebrew tool pilot.  
3. Let M9 use LDP's mentoring and labs, and supply what LDP lacks: biblical commissioning with laying on of hands (Acts 13:3; 1 Tim 4:14).  
4. Name Faircreek's Statement of Faith as the governing guardrail, with Nicene and WCF as reference, and use LDP's core vs peripheral tool.  
5. Add \#11 Generosity and \#12 Witness and service. Faircreek already counts them.  
6. Point the leader-selection section at the Leadership Agreement for character.

## Glossary

- **Align:** Faircreek's twice-yearly gathering of church leaders for training, celebration, evaluation, worship and prayer; LDP commencement happens there.  
- **Core Beliefs Assessment:** LDP's 20-statement exercise rating beliefs 1 (essential) to 3 (fine to differ).  
- **Discipleship Flywheel:** Faircreek's house graphic drawn as a cycle: Foyer, Living Room, Kitchen, Neighborhood.  
- **Five Faith Catalysts:** Andy Stanley's five ways God grows faith: practical teaching, private disciplines, personal ministry, providential relationships, pivotal circumstances.  
- **Leadership Agreement:** the covenant Faircreek leaders sign: belief, practice, relationships, daily decisions, direction.  
- **Leadership Posture:** Faircreek's four leader traits: Humble, Intentional, Teachable, FOR.  
- **Learning Lab:** LDP's hands-on month serving in a Faircreek ministry.  
- **LDG, LDP, OIA, Pathway B, WCF:** see Terms used here.
