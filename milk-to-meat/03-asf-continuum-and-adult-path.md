# **ASF milk to meat continuum and adult path**

Nov 10, 2026 · Steven Thorsen

## **BLUF**

**BLUF:** Faircreek should adopt a five-stage adult path (Explore, Foundations, Rooted, Mature, Multiplying) laid over the house pathway already in ASF Version 4, and pilot it with one Foundations cohort during Lent 2027 that ends in Easter baptisms on March 28\. Hebrews 5 and 6 supply the spine: milk has a defined content (the six foundations in Hebrews 6:1-2), solid food is defined by trained discernment rather than harder topics (Hebrews 5:14), and maturity is measured by whether a believer can teach others (Hebrews 5:12). Faircreek's current adult offerings (Community Groups, Circle Up, Dwell, the weekly message tools, LDP) already cover pieces of stages 1, 3 and 5; the clear gaps are a defined Foundations track, a solid-food track for men, and mentors who walk people from one stage to the next.

Adult Spiritual Formation (ASF) is the working name for this effort. The doc builds on ASF Version 4 and Matt's June list of current activities; it doesn't redo the source comparisons.

**Update, 14 Nov 2026:** the milk-to-meat briefing deck and the ASF Milk-to-Meat Continuum Map now carry the detail behind this path: the gospel first, as Starting Point; two pathways (new believers and long-time members); nine meat modules; and LDP, Faircreek's Leadership Development Program, as the leader track with a meat module braided into each of its modules. The five stages here line up with the map's steps: Explore is Step 0 (the gospel), Foundations is milk, Rooted is practice (growing), Mature is meat, and Multiplying is sent. Two things in this draft have moved on. And the Lent 2027 pilot is now Starting Point, How to Follow Jesus and "Baptism and the Table," ending in Easter baptisms. ## **The milk to meat continuum**

The continuum has five stages, and each one names what grace is doing, what a believer at that stage looks like, and the signal that it's time to move on. Three texts set its logic:

* **Milk has content.** "As newborn babes, desire the pure milk of the word, that you may grow thereby" (1 Peter 2:2). Hebrews 6:1-2 lists that milk: "repentance from dead works and of faith toward God, of the doctrine of baptisms, of laying on of hands, of resurrection of the dead, and of eternal judgment."  
* **Immaturity shows in conduct, not only in knowledge.** "I fed you with milk and not with solid food; for until now you were not able to receive it, and even now you are still not able; for you are still carnal. For where there are envy, strife, and divisions among you, are you not carnal and behaving like mere men?" (1 Corinthians 3:2-3).  
* **Solid food is trained discernment, and the goal is teaching.** "For though by this time you ought to be teachers, you need someone to teach you again the first principles of the oracles of God ... But solid food belongs to those who are of full age, that is, those who by reason of use have their senses exercised to discern both good and evil" (Hebrews 5:12, 14).

So the path isn't a ladder of harder classes. It moves a person from receiving the foundations, to practicing them in community, to discerning and serving, to teaching others (2 Timothy 2:2). It mirrors the early church's catechumenate, which moved inquirers through inquiry, instruction, baptism and post-baptism teaching with a sponsor at each step.

| Stage | House room | Believer's marks | Core content | Practices | Entry signal | Exit signal |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 1\. Explore ("come and see," John 1:46) | Foyer | Seeker, returner, new attender, or wounded Christian; questions outnumber convictions | Who Jesus is; the gospel; what the church is | Sunday worship; one honest conversation with a guide | Shows up, asks a question, fills out a connect card | Professes faith or recommits, and asks "what's next?" |
| 2\. Foundations (milk) | Foyer to Living Room | New or newly serious believer; eager but easily unsettled (Ephesians 4:14) | The six foundations of Hebrews 6:1-2; assurance; how to read the Bible and pray | Daily Bible reading and prayer; joins a group; takes the Lord's Supper | Profession of faith, recommitment, or request for baptism | Baptized; in a group; can explain the gospel in their own words; a daily rhythm most days for about 3 months |
| 3\. Rooted | Living Room | Known by name and story; habits forming; still working through envy, strife and self-protection (1 Corinthians 3:3) | The whole-Bible story; core doctrine; a rule of life; prayer as communion | A simple rule of life; confession and care in group; regular giving; serving monthly | Exit signals of stage 2 | Owns a rule of life; serves regularly; can study a book of the Bible with some help; reconciles rather than divides |
| 4\. Mature (solid food) | Kitchen | Steady through trial; discerns good and evil in gray areas (Hebrews 5:14); serves without being asked | Systematic doctrine; book-length study (Hebrews, Romans); apologetics; suffering, ethics, vocation | Sabbath, fasting, solitude; mentors one younger believer; leads discussion | Exit signals of stage 3, and a leader's invitation | Mentors someone well; shows character before competency; ready for leader training |
| 5\. Multiplying (teachers, Hebrews 5:12) | Kitchen to Neighborhood | Leads and shepherds; reproduces faith in others (2 Timothy 2:2) | Faircreek's LDP leader course; teaching and shepherding skills; pastoral care and referral | Leads a group or class; apprentices a successor; ongoing leader huddles | Exit signals of stage 4, and church affirmation | No exit; the signal is an apprentice ready to lead |

**Mission runs through every stage.** The Neighborhood room isn't a final stage. A stage 1 believer invites a friend; a stage 3 believer hosts a meal; a stage 5 believer equips others for witness. Each stage should carry one witness practice sized to it.

**Grace guardrail.** Stages describe growth, not spiritual status. People move at different speeds, sometimes backward in hard seasons, and no stage earns standing with God (ASF Version 3 guardrails).

## **Draft adult curriculum by stage**

Each stage gets one core track that every adult at that stage is invited into, plus electives drawn from the sources ASF already vetted. Core tracks run as short cohorts (6 to 8 weeks) so people can start several times a year.

| Stage | Core track (draft) | Electives and resources | Length |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 1\. Explore | North Point's Starting Point, a small-group course for seekers and returners (Matt already uses North Point's partner resources) | BibleProject overview videos on the gospel and the story of the Bible | 8 to 10 weeks |
| 2\. Foundations | **Foundations**, built in-house, one week per Hebrews 6:1-2 foundation: (1) repentance from dead works, (2) faith toward God, (3) baptisms, covering baptism and the Lord's Supper, (4) laying on of hands, covering the Holy Spirit, gifts and commissioning, (5) resurrection of the dead, (6) eternal judgment and assurance | Circle Up's "Praying That Feels Real"; R.C. Sproul's *Essential Truths of the Christian Faith* as a reader; BibleProject's How to Read the Bible series | 6 weeks |
| 3\. Rooted | **Rule of life** cohort using the Practicing the Way course, framed by the Version 3 grace guardrails | BibleProject whole-Bible reading plan; Tim Keller's *Prayer* as a group study; North Point group studies from groupleaders.org; Dwell book studies | 8 weeks, then ongoing in a Community Group |
| 4\. Mature | **Deep study** cohort: one book-length study (Hebrews or Romans) paired with a systematic doctrine series from Ligonier (Sproul) or the Westminster Shorter Catechism | Keller's *The Reason for God* (apologetics); Sabbath, fasting and solitude practice from Practicing the Way; suffering and vocation studies | 10 to 12 weeks |
| 5\. Multiplying | Faircreek's **LDP** leader course | groupleaders.org Learning Labs (Matt flagged these in August); leader huddles built on the loop: build trust, be a guide, cultivate community, develop depth | Per LDP, then monthly huddles |

Two design notes:

* **Foundations is the missing piece.** Nothing Faircreek runs today owns the milk stage on purpose, so it's the one track worth writing in-house. Hebrews 6:1-2 hands over the syllabus.  
* **Doctrine travels with practice.** Practicing the Way supplies the practices; Sproul and the Westminster Confession of Faith (WCF) keep the grammar of grace and sanctification straight; Keller keeps prayer from becoming a self-improvement project. That's the Version 2 and 3 balance, applied stage by stage.

## **Folding in what Faircreek runs now**

None of today's adult activities need to stop; each gets a stage, a purpose and one change so it feeds the path instead of standing alone. The list comes from Matt's June 2 note and the January 2026 Circle Up and Dwell announcement.

| Current activity | Stage it serves | Change to make it part of the path |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Weekly message tools: YouVersion notes and reflection questions, app discussion questions, 5-day devotional | All stages (the shared weekly rhythm) | Keep as the common spine. Add one "go deeper" item each week for stages 3 and 4\. |
| Community Groups, including men's and women's groups | 2 and 3 (Living Room) | Run as communities of practice, not only discussion. Each group knows where its members are and picks its next study by stage. |
| Circle Up short-term groups (3 weeks, a few times a year) | 2 to 4, by topic | Use as the on-ramp and elective slot. Tag each topic: "Praying That Feels Real" is stage 2; parenting and the "Lies Men/Women Believe" studies are stage 3; prophecy ("Is the End Near?") is stage 4\. Host the Foundations pilot in a Circle Up slot. |
| Dwell women's book studies (7 weeks, James) | 3 and 4 | Recognize as the women's solid-food option. Build a matching men's track, since none exists today. |
| Starting Point (on Faircreek's "For the One in 2026" house graphic, in the Foyer) | 1 (Foyer) | Make it the standard first step after a connect card: the gospel comes first, before any milk (topic 0 on the continuum map). |
| Serving and volunteer systems (Matt's area) | 3 to 5 (Kitchen) | Tie each serving role to a formation goal so serving becomes discipleship, not staffing. |
| LDP leader course | 5 | Make it the stage 5 core track, entered by invitation after stage 4 signals. The manual (read 14 Nov) shows it's what the Discipleship Flywheel calls Leadership Development Groups. Phases 2 and 3 are stage 5 work; Phase 1 (Bible method, gifts, prayer) is really stage 3 to 4 content. It teaches almost none of the Hebrews 6:1–2 foundations, so braid one meat module into each LDP module and turn its commencement at Align into commissioning (see the LDP Milk-to-Meat Transformation Map). |

**Gaps the path exposes:** no defined Foundations track; no men's equivalent to Dwell; no mentor or sponsor who walks a person between stages; no simple way for an adult to see where they are and what's next; and no training for everyday witness.

## **Way forward**

The plan runs in four phases over about ten months, starting with adults and one small pilot rather than a church-wide launch. It follows the Version 4 sequence (map, train, pilot) and times the pilot to the church year.

1. **Agree and map (Nov 16 to Dec 2026).** Settle the stages and names on Monday. Map every adult activity onto the stages (the table above is a first pass) and list what each stage lacks. Output: a one-page pathway map Greg and Matt can both endorse.  
2. **Design (Jan to early Feb 2027).** Write the 6-week Foundations track and its leader guide. Build a short self-placement tool (about 10 questions plus a conversation with a guide). Recruit and brief 4 to 6 mentors from stage 4 and 5 adults. Output: Foundations ready to teach; mentors named.  
3. **Pilot (Lent, Feb 10 to Mar 28, 2027).** Run one Foundations cohort of 8 to 12 adults in a Circle Up slot, with a mentor for each pair, ending in baptisms on Easter Sunday, March 28\. In parallel, have 2 or 3 Community Groups try a stage 3 rule of life, and invite a handful of men into a stage 4 deep-study cohort. Output: stories, attendance, and what participants say helped.  
4. **Evaluate and widen (Apr to Aug 2027).** Revise the tracks, publish the pathway map, wire LDP to the stage 4 exit signals, and plan a fall 2027 rollout, perhaps alongside a sermon series on Hebrews 5 and 6\.

**People needed for the pilot:** an ASF lead to own the path; Matt as owner of groups and next steps; Greg's endorsement and pulpit support; 2 Foundations facilitators; 4 to 6 mentors; and the group leaders whose groups pilot the rule of life.

**How to tell it's working:** the Version 4 fruit measures, applied per stage. Watch for people taking a next step, baptisms, adults known in a group, practices kept, service, reconciliation, generosity, new leaders, and witness stories. Count gently, since the path must never become a ladder of status.
